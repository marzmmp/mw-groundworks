Spring — Mar to May

Clean-ups, mulch, and the first cuts of the season. Worst window for land clearing — the ground is mud and equipment ruts it.

Summer — Jun to Aug

Mowing settles into rhythm as fescue slows in the heat. Prime season for walls, masonry and decks.

Fall & winter — Sep to Feb

Aerate and overseed in early September, leaves off by November — then clearing season opens. Frozen ground, dormant growth, clear sightlines.
